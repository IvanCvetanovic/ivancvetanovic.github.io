Support

Redma! teaches the German dialects of Austria, Germany and Switzerland — conversation lessons, a dialect map and a survival phrasebook, all working offline on your device.

Report a wrong dialect phrase

Packs marked Preview are seed content waiting for native-speaker review, so a line can be off — a word nobody says in that region, a wrong article, a translation that misses the tone. Those reports are the single most useful thing you can send.

  1. Open Profile → About & Licenses and tap Report a wrong phrase. The mail arrives already labelled with the dialect you are learning.
  2. Paste the phrase as the app shows it, and write what a local would actually say.
  3. If you can, say which region you speak for — Vienna and Graz disagree plenty.

Corrections go into a later content update.

Volunteer as a native speaker

Most of Redma!'s dialect packs have not yet been reviewed by native speakers, and that is where you can help. If you speak one of the dialects and want to review a pack, or help add recorded audio in future, mail us — the Contribute to Redma! card on your profile writes that mail for you.

Anything else

  • Progress disappeared — progress is stored on the device and there is no account to restore from, by design. It only comes back after a reinstall or on a new phone if your phone's own system backup included it.
  • Nothing is spoken aloud — Redma! uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine. Check that a German voice is installed under your system speech settings, and that the phone is not muted.
  • Privacy — see the Privacy Policy: the app collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

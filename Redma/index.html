Redma!

Learn the dialects people actually speak — Wienerisch, Bairisch, Züritüütsch, Kölsch and more, across Austria, Germany and Switzerland.

What it is

Redma! is a mobile app for expats and international students in the German-speaking countries, where textbook German stops working the moment you leave the classroom. Pick a region on an interactive dialect map, learn it through short conversation lessons with Standard German glosses, phonetic hints and cultural notes, then check yourself with a quiz. A searchable survival guide covers the moments you need a phrase right now.

Local-first, by design

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Your progress lives on your device and is never uploaded.

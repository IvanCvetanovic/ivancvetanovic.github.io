Privacy Policy

Redma! · Last updated 15 September 2026

Redma! has no accounts, no servers of its own and makes no network requests. Nothing you do in the app is collected, sent anywhere or shared with anyone.

What the app stores

On your device

Your display name, chosen dialect, XP, day streak, per-dialect lesson progress, your place in a lesson you haven't finished, the words you marked as learned, the day you last earned practice XP, starred survival-guide phrases, your light/dark choice and whether you finished onboarding. All of it is written to the operating system's app-private storage on this device. The app contains no code that uploads any of it.

What it never touches

There is no account or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and no third-party service that receives anything about you. The open-source packages the app is built from — listed under About & Licenses inside the app — all run on the device. The app asks for no runtime permissions — no microphone, camera, location, contacts or photo access — and reads no device or advertising identifiers.

Speech

Text-to-speech

Lesson lines and vocabulary are handed to your operating system's own text-to-speech engine to be spoken aloud. The app does not transmit that text anywhere itself. Some system speech engines synthesise on the device and others do it on their maker's servers — which yours does is governed by your device maker's privacy policy and your system speech settings, not by this app.

Deleting your data

Reset Progress

Profile → Reset Progress clears your XP, day streak, per-dialect lesson progress, learned words, any half-finished lesson and the day you last earned practice XP. Everything else — your name, chosen dialect, starred phrases and app settings — is kept.

Uninstalling

Uninstalling Redma! removes everything the app stored on this device, because all of it lives in the app's private storage. There is no copy on a server for us to delete — there never was one.

Beyond the app

Google Play and the App Store

If you installed Redma! from a store, Apple or Google records the install on their side, and receives crash reports if you have that switched on for your device. That happens between you and them, independently of this app, and is covered by their privacy policies rather than this one.

Your phone's own backup

If system backup is switched on for your phone, Android (or iCloud, on an iPhone) may include this app's stored data in the backup kept in your own Google or Apple account, and restore it when you set up a new phone or reinstall. That backup is made by the operating system, not by Redma!, and we never receive it. You can switch it off in your system settings.

Changes

If this ever stops being true

This policy describes version 1.0. If a later version sends anything off the device itself — accounts, sync, a backup of its own — this page and the copy inside the app are updated before that version ships.

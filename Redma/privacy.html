Privacy Policy

Redma! · Last updated 11 October 2026

Redma! has no accounts and no servers of its own, and your progress never leaves your device. The only network traffic comes from Google: the occasional ad, and Google Play handling the optional "Remove ads" purchase.

What the app stores

On your device

Your display name, chosen dialect, XP, day streak, per-dialect lesson progress, your place in a lesson you haven't finished, the words you marked as learned, the day you last earned practice XP, starred survival-guide phrases, your light/dark choice, whether you finished onboarding, whether you bought "Remove ads", and a few counters that keep ads rare (which lessons have been counted and when an ad last appeared), and your ad-consent choice, which Google's consent tool saves. All of it is written to the operating system's app-private storage on this device. Redma!'s own code uploads none of it.

What it doesn't do

There is no account or sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of Redma!'s own, and no profile of you kept by Redma!. The open-source packages the app is built from — listed under About & Licenses inside the app — run on the device; the ones that reach a network are Google's ad SDK and Google Play's billing library, described below. The app asks for no runtime permissions — no microphone, camera, location, contacts or photo access — and does not itself read any device identifier. The ad SDK does, as explained next.

Ads and purchases

Ads (Google AdMob)

Redma! is free because it shows an occasional full-screen ad from Google AdMob: at most one after every third new lesson you finish, never within 20 minutes of the last one and never in your first two days. To show it, Google's ad SDK contacts Google's servers. According to Google, it collects your IP address (which can indicate your general location), how you interact with ads such as taps and video views, diagnostic information, and device and account identifiers including the Android advertising ID, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Redma! never sees that data and sends Google nothing about what you learn. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings.

Your ad choices (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before any ad is requested, and you can change it at any time under Profile → Ad privacy choices. If you do not consent to personalised ads, Google may still show ads that are not personalised.

Remove ads (Google Play)

This is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play: Google, not Redma!, receives your payment details. Redma! only learns from Google Play, on your device, that you own it, and keeps a yes/no flag. Google's billing library may also report technical information about the purchase to Google. Once you own it Redma! stops requesting ads and no longer asks Google for ad consent. On a new phone, use Profile → Restore purchase.

Speech

Text-to-speech

Lesson lines and vocabulary are handed to your operating system's own text-to-speech engine to be spoken aloud. The app does not transmit that text anywhere itself. Some system speech engines synthesise on the device and others do it on their maker's servers — which yours does is governed by your device maker's privacy policy and your system speech settings, not by this app.

Deleting your data

Reset Progress

Profile → Reset Progress clears your XP, day streak, per-dialect lesson progress, learned words, any half-finished lesson and the day you last earned practice XP. Everything else — your name, chosen dialect, starred phrases, app settings, a "Remove ads" purchase and the counters that keep ads rare — is kept.

Uninstalling

Uninstalling Redma! removes everything the app stored on this device, because all of it lives in the app's private storage. There is no copy on a server for us to delete — there never was one. Data Google collects through ads is held by Google and covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Beyond the app

Google Play and the App Store

If you installed Redma! from a store, Apple or Google records the install on their side, and receives crash reports if you have that switched on for your device. That happens between you and them, independently of this app, and is covered by their privacy policies rather than this one.

Your phone's own backup

If system backup is switched on for your phone, Android (or iCloud, on an iPhone) may include this app's stored data in the backup kept in your own Google or Apple account, and restore it when you set up a new phone or reinstall. That backup is made by the operating system, not by Redma!, and we never receive it. You can switch it off in your system settings.

Changes

If this ever stops being true

This policy describes version 1.1, the first with ads and the "Remove ads" purchase. If a later version sends anything off the device itself in a new way — accounts, sync, a backup of its own — this page and the copy inside the app are updated before that version ships.

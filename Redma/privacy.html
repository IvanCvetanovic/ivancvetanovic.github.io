Privacy Policy

Redma! · Last updated 12 October 2026

Redma! has no user accounts, and I do not run any servers that receive your data. The app never uploads your progress. The only network traffic comes from Google: the occasional ad, and Google Play handling the optional "Remove ads" purchase. Your phone's own backup and speech engine can also move data, as explained below.

Summary

  • Everything you do in the app (progress, settings, starred phrases) is stored on your phone. The app never uploads it, and I never receive it.
  • The free app shows an occasional full-screen ad through Google AdMob. Google's ad software may use your advertising ID, your IP address and how you interact with ads.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before an ad is requested.
  • Removing ads is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. I never see your payment details.
  • If Android backup is on, Google may keep a copy of the app's data in your Google account. Android may keep that copy after you uninstall the app.
  • The app asks for no permission that Android would ask you to approve: no microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.

What Redma! Stores on Your Device

Your display name, chosen dialect, XP, day streak, per-dialect lesson progress, your place in a lesson you haven't finished, the words you marked as learned, the day you last earned practice XP, starred survival-guide phrases, your light/dark choice and whether you finished onboarding. It also keeps whether you bought "Remove ads", a few counters that keep ads rare (which lessons have been counted, the day of the first lesson the app counted and when an ad last appeared), and your ad-consent choice, which Google's consent tool saves. All of it is written to the app's private storage on your device. Redma!'s own code uploads none of it.

The feedback, volunteer and dialect-correction buttons open your own email app with my address and a subject line filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.

What Redma! Does Not Do

There is no account or sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting library of Redma!'s own, and no profile of you kept by Redma!. The open-source packages the app is built from (listed under About & Licenses inside the app) run on your device; the ones that reach a network are Google's ad library and Google Play's billing library, described below. The app asks for no runtime permission (no microphone, camera, location, contacts or photo access) and does not itself read any device identifier. Its manifest does declare the permissions those two Google libraries need: internet access, network state, the advertising ID and other Google ad-service permissions, Google Play purchases, and wake lock and foreground service for background work. Google's ad library reads the advertising ID, as explained next.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Redma! is free because it shows an occasional full-screen ad from Google AdMob. An ad can appear when you tap Back to Quests after finishing a lesson: at most one ad after every third lesson the app counts, never within 20 minutes of the previous ad, and never on the day of the first lesson the app counts or on the day after. The app counts a lesson the first time you finish it in version 1.1 (so a lesson you finished in version 1.0 counts once, when you next finish it), and a lesson never counts twice, not even after Reset Progress. The button reads "Ad starting…" just before an ad appears.

To have the ad ready, Google's ad library contacts Google's servers in the background while you do the quiz of a lesson that would make an ad due. It makes no ad request during other lessons. Google's library also reports when an ad was shown or tapped. If such a report cannot be sent because you are offline, the library may retry it in the background once a connection returns, even if the app is closed.

According to Google, its ad library collects your IP address (which can indicate your general location), how you interact with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views, diagnostic information, and device and account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and an app set ID, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google and its advertising partners may use this information to show, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. Redma! never sees that data and sends Google nothing about what you learn: the ad request contains no lesson information or keywords.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (usually under Settings > Google > Ads; the exact path depends on your device).

More information about how Google uses data in ads and in apps that use its services:

  • https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Your Ad Choices (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Google's consent form (its User Messaging Platform) is shown where Google requires it, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Where it is shown, it asks for your choice before an ad is requested, and the advertising partners that may receive data are listed in it.

Redma! does not contact Google's consent service on the day of the first lesson the app counts or on the day after. From the second day after that first lesson's day, and unless you have removed ads, it asks Google's consent service whether a consent form is needed. It does this for every user, wherever you are, because Google decides from the request where you are. The check can run when you start the quiz of a lesson that would make an ad due, when you tap Back to Quests (once per session, or whenever an ad is due), and when you open Profile.

You can reopen the form and change your choice at any time under Profile → Ad privacy choices. That row appears only where Google requires it, only after those first two days, and not once you have removed ads. If you do not consent to personalised ads, Google may still show ads that are not personalised.

Remove Ads (Google Play Billing)

Remove ads is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Google, not Redma!, receives your payment details; I do not receive or store them. Google Play may show me purchase records, such as an order ID, in the Play Console. Redma! only learns from Google Play, on your device, that you own it, and keeps a yes/no flag.

Each time the app starts, Redma! asks Google Play (through Google's billing library and the Play Store app on your phone) which purchases your Google account owns. That is how a purchase made on another phone, or finished while the app was closed, is found. When you open Profile, it also asks Google Play what Remove ads costs in your country. Google's billing library may also report technical information about the purchase to Google. You can also use Profile → Restore purchase.

Once you own it, Redma! stops requesting ads and no longer asks Google for ad consent. For more information, see https://policies.google.com/privacy

Speech

Lesson lines and vocabulary are handed to your operating system's own text-to-speech engine to be spoken aloud. The app does not transmit that text anywhere itself. Some system speech engines synthesise on the device and others do it on their maker's servers; which yours does is governed by your device maker's privacy policy and your system speech settings, not by this app.

Deleting Your Data

Profile → Reset Progress clears your XP, day streak, per-dialect lesson progress, learned words, any half-finished lesson and the day you last earned practice XP. Everything else (your name, chosen dialect, starred phrases, app settings, the "Remove ads" flag, the ad counters and your ad-consent choice) is kept.

Uninstalling Redma! removes everything the app stored on your device, and I hold no copy on a server. Two things can remain outside the app, and I cannot delete either: a backup copy that Android may keep in your Google account (see the next section), and data that Google collected through ads and purchases, which Google holds under its own privacy policy.

Your Phone's Own Backup

I have set no backup rules, so Android's automatic backup applies in its default form. If backup is switched on for your phone, Android may copy the app's stored data (everything listed above, including the "Remove ads" flag, your saved ad-consent choice and whatever Google's ad and consent libraries keep there) to your Google account, and restore it when you set up a new phone or reinstall the app. When you move to a new phone by cable or wireless transfer, Android may also copy it directly from the old phone. That backup is made by Android, not by Redma!, and I never receive it. Android may keep the copy in your Google account after you uninstall the app; you can manage or delete backups, or switch backup off, in your Android or Google backup settings.

Google Play

If you installed Redma! from Google Play, Google records the install on its side, and receives crash reports if you have that switched on for your device. That happens between you and Google, independently of this app, and is covered by Google's privacy policy rather than this one. Google Play may also show me anonymised crash reports and install statistics in the Play Console.

Children's Privacy

Redma! is intended for people aged 16 and over. It is not intended for children under 16 (or under the minimum age required in your country), and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices and Rights

I do not run a server or user accounts that receive your data. Google (AdMob and Google Play) receives the data described above. You can delete your progress on your device at any time with Profile → Reset Progress (it keeps your name, chosen dialect, starred phrases, app settings, the "Remove ads" flag, the ad counters and your ad-consent choice), and everything else the app stores by clearing its storage in Android settings or by uninstalling it. Data held by Google is governed by Google's own policies and tools.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland: personalised advertising is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time under Profile → Ad privacy choices. Data may be processed by Google outside your country. You have the right to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information about you, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority (in the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner's Office). Because I hold no personal information about you myself, apart from purchase records that Google Play shows me, requests about data held by Google usually need to go to Google, but you can contact me with any question at the address below.

Data Security

Redma!'s own code makes no network requests. According to Google, all data its ad library collects is encrypted in transit with TLS, and purchases go through Google Play. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy describes version 1.1, the first with ads and the "Remove ads" purchase. I may update it from time to time; changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date, and the copy inside the app (Profile → Privacy) will be updated to match. For material changes I will also say so in the update notes on Google Play. If a later version sends anything off the device itself in a new way (accounts, sync, a backup of its own), this page and the copy inside the app are updated before that version ships.
